WebXR for Meta Quest 3

Jab & Jump

A virtual jump rope that turns when you circle your wrists. Every time the rope slaps the floor your controllers kick, so you feel the rhythm.

ReadyJump rope HIIT

Sprints, double-under bursts and rest breaks set to generated beats, with a voice coach calling each change. Free mode gives you a steady timed round.

ReadyNunchucks

One real-physics nunchuck. Whip the free stick to smash boards, and squeeze grip with your empty hand near the stick to catch it and switch hands.

ReadyKung fu

Fight your way up a temple, floor by floor. Punch fighters from every side, swat knives back at their throwers, and block, duck or jump the boss.

ReadyRingside Rumble

Box your way up a ladder of four original fighters. Read each tell, lean or duck to dodge, then counter. Squeeze grip as you punch to throw a star punch.

How to play and stay safe
  • Clear about 2 × 2 m of floor and put on the controller wrist straps.
  • Hold the controllers like rope handles and make small forward circles with your wrists.
  • Jump as the rope comes down to your feet. You'll feel a buzz on every floor hit and a long buzz on a trip.
  • Touch a pad with your hand to choose. Hold both triggers for 1 second to end a round early.
  • Press the Meta button on the right controller to leave.
